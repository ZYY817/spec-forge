# Requirement Anchor / 需求定锚器

> 把纷杂的输入，锚定为清晰的判断与可执行的共识。  
> From scattered context to grounded requirements.

**Requirement Anchor** is a reusable Codex skill for turning meetings, conversations, screenshots, files, and unfinished ideas into requirements that people can understand, build, and verify.

## 它从哪里来 / Where It Comes From

这个 skill 起源于一套日常工作中的个人实践：面对会议记录、聊天片段和零散想法，先辨认真正的问题，再厘清已经确认的事实，区分讨论中的方案与最终结论，最后将理解沉淀为可以执行和验收的需求。

It grew from an everyday working practice: understanding the real problem behind messy context, separating facts from assumptions, and shaping conversations into requirements that teams can build and validate.

它不是某个公司的专属流程，也不是一套必须照搬的模板。你可以直接使用，也可以按照自己的团队、项目和思考方式重新塑造它。

It is not tied to any company or rigid process. Use it as a starting point, adapt it to your team and projects, and make it your own.

## 它解决什么问题 / What It Helps With

需求往往不是从一张白纸开始，而是从一场没有结论的会议、一段不完整的对话，或一个还没有被说清楚的想法开始。

Requirements rarely begin as a blank page. They begin as an unfinished conversation, a noisy meeting, or an idea that has not yet found its shape.

Requirement Anchor helps you:

- 从零散材料中找到真正需要解决的问题；
- 区分事实、结论、假设、待确认事项和后续规划；
- 识别会议转录中的错词、断句和术语问题；
- 根据实际复杂度组织需求，而不是机械套用模板；
- 将需求写到产品、开发和测试都能理解和验证的程度。

It helps you:

- find the real problem behind scattered material;
- separate facts, decisions, assumptions, open questions, and future plans;
- interpret noisy transcripts and correct ambiguous terminology through context;
- choose an appropriate form instead of forcing every request into one template;
- produce requirements that product, engineering, and QA can align on and verify.

## 适用场景 / Use Cases

- 会议记录、聊天记录、截图或自由描述转需求；
- 产品、运营、后台和技术优化需求；
- 已有需求的评审、重写和补充；
- 复杂讨论中的结论提取与范围梳理；
- 需要时补充开发、测试和实现规划。

## 核心原则 / Core Principles

**先理解，再成文。**  
**Understand first, write second.**

不把猜测写成事实，不把建议写成结论，不把未来规划混进当前交付范围。需求的价值，不只在于写得完整，更在于让不同角色对同一件事形成可靠的理解。

Do not turn guesses into facts, suggestions into decisions, or future plans into current scope. A good requirement is not merely complete; it creates a reliable shared understanding.

## 语言 / Language

支持中文和英文。用户可以直接指定输出语言；如果没有指定，skill 会跟随输入材料的主要语言。

Supports both Chinese and English. Specify the output language when needed; otherwise, the skill follows the primary language of the input.

## 自定义 / Make It Your Own

你可以修改 `SKILL.md` 和 `references/` 下的内容，加入自己的需求字段、团队术语、评审规则和输出偏好。它更像一枚可以不断校准的锚，而不是一块固定不变的模板。

You can customize `SKILL.md` and the files under `references/` with your own fields, terminology, review rules, and writing preferences. Think of it as an anchor you can keep calibrating—not a template carved in stone.
